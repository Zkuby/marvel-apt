# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, static export for Cloudflare Pages, separate GitHub-ready project (IDEA.md).

## Product Purpose

Lithuanian-first website with an English home page for Marvel apartamentai in Kaunas. Let prospective guests see the actual apartment, find guest feedback and try choosing dates and guest count.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Reservation is a transparent demo, with no live availability, payments or submissions. Keep copy short, use real photos and attributed review material. Show ratings and a count in the opening view. Total refers to the publicly confirmed source counts only, not unique guests.

## Brand Commitments

Modern, welcoming and quietly luxurious. Original apartment photography and name remain. Mobile and reduced-motion access are part of the deliverable.

## Users

Assumption from the brief and property listings: Lithuanian-speaking visitors, including families and groups planning a stay in Kaunas.
